Sustainable Garden Foundation  ·  Our Mission

Why we exist.

One mission, stated plainly — and the three commitments that carry it.

Mission Statement

Dignity is not contingent on wealth

The Sustainable Garden Foundation exists to prove — in lived, everyday reality — that dignity, ecological restoration, and shared abundance are not utopian ideals but achievable design outcomes.

Our mission is to design, seed, and support a federation of self-sufficient Oasis communities on land scarred by wildfire and disaster: communities governed by consensus, powered by clean energy, and rooted in the Ubuntu principle — I am because we are.

“OASIS communities are an answer to despair: a place where the future has already arrived, where dignity is not contingent on wealth.”
— Guy J.L. Vallières, Founder

We measure our success not by growth for its own sake, but by whether every person who joins an Oasis finds what despair had told them no longer existed: a home, a purpose, and a future already under construction.

How We Carry It Out

Three commitments

Every SGF initiative — the Oasis Project, the Blue Dot Project, the Ready and Easy invention families — is a direct expression of one of the three commitments below.

Commitment 01

Restore the Land

We build on wasteland and disaster-affected sites — reducing land costs, maximising community buy-in, and healing degraded ecosystems as a precondition of settlement, not an afterthought.

Commitment 02

Restore Dignity

Every design decision — housing, governance, invention, employment — is filtered through one question: does this preserve the dignity of the person it serves?

Commitment 03

Prove the Model

We are not proposing theory. The first Oasis is a proof of concept — measurable, replicable, and designed from day one to seed the next one.

A significant share of licensing and sales revenue from SGF's own inventions — the Ready and Easy product families — is directed back into the Foundation, directly funding this mission.
